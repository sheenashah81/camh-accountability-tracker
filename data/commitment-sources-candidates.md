## How candidates were found (method, for repeatability)
1. Start from the country's own mental health law, policy or national plan, because governments publish these.
2. Search the exact document title plus the country, then prefer results from government domains, WHO or UN pages and legislation databases over news.
3. Cross-check with a second source, such as the WHO MiNDbank record or a national legislation database, to confirm that the title and year are right.
4. Record unclear points as "unconfirmed" instead of filling gaps.
5. A person opens the primary document and verifies the commitment before a record is created.
