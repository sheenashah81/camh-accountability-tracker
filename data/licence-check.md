# Licence check for commitment sources (task 3.3)

Checked 2026-10-27 from pages opened by the project author. The repository stores only links, titles and short paraphrases of public documents, no long extracts. This is a practical check, not legal advice.

| Country | Source | Reuse terms found | Recorded as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kenya | Mental Health Policy 2015-2030 | Printed statement: any part may be freely reviewed, quoted, reproduced or translated if the source is acknowledged; not to be sold or used commercially | Other, see note (non-commercial) |
| South Africa | National Mental Health Policy Framework 2023-2030 | Department of Health website terms: view, copy, download, print and distribute for non-commercial, informational or reference purposes; commercial use needs written permission | Other, see note (non-commercial) |
| United Kingdom | 10 Year Health Plan for England | GOV.UK footer: Open Government Licence v3.0 except where stated; Crown copyright 2025 | Open Government Licence v3 |
| Nigeria | National Mental Health Act 2021 | No reuse licence stated. Ministry policies page lists the Act but the download returns "page missing" (checked 2026-10-28); only the Gazette copy is accessible | Other, see note (link and short paraphrase only) |
| Bangladesh | National Mental Health Strategic Plan 2020-2030, and National Plan of Action for Adolescent Health 2017-2030 (second record) | No reuse licence stated on the pages checked (DGHS, NIMH, DGFP) | Other, see note (link and short paraphrase only) |
| Jordan | National Mental Health Policy 2011 | No reuse licence stated in the sources reached; Ministry copyright page not accessible; cited copy is held by the Mental Health Innovation Network | Other, see note (link and short paraphrase only) |
| Philippines | Republic Act 11036 (Senate record) | Statute: Intellectual Property Code (RA 8293) s.175 excludes official legislative texts from copyright; Senate PDF blocks automated access | Public domain (official legislative text) |
| Colombia | Ley 2460 de 2025 | Funcion Publica terms: use subject to intellectual property rules; automated copying of content prohibited without consent; no open licence | Other, see note (link and short paraphrase only) |

**Effect.** A record with "Not yet checked" cannot be Published (rule P1). Five records stay in Draft until a licence or reuse statement is found, or the item is shown as a link with a short factual summary only, which does not need reuse rights. The two non-commercial terms suit this non-commercial prototype and must be repeated on any page that displays those records.
